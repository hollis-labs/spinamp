## What you can do
- Use, study, modify and redistribute the code under the MIT License.
- Refer to the project by name accurately: "based on Spinamp", "compatible with Spinamp", a link to

- Distribute an unmodified build and call it Spinamp, if you point users to the official project
  and do not charge for the name.

## What we ask you not to do
- Call a modified version, fork or derivative "Spinamp", or use a confusingly similar name, logo or
  icon. Please give your version its own name and artwork.
- Suggest that your product, service, site or domain is made, endorsed or sponsored by Hollis Labs or
  is the official Spinamp. In particular, please do not register domains, app-store listings or social
  accounts that use "Spinamp" or "spinamp.fm" in a way that could be mistaken for ours.
- Use the logo or icon as the identity of another project.
